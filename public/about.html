About Me

Hi, I'm Ofir

Since I can remember, I've always loved creating and thinking about unique solutions. My first "invention" was in second grade - I kept a sketchbook where I wrote down all my ideas.

When I discovered industrial design, I immediately knew it was the right path for me. I enjoy understanding how things work and designing solutions for people while considering their environment.

As a designer, I believe empathy is one of our most important tools. I listen before I design, trying to understand people's experiences and needs before creating solutions.

Beyond design, I'm a maker at heart. I love building and experimenting with DIY projects, and I'm always excited to learn something new.
