MEMORE

A mobile app for memorial events, shared memories, and community support during grief.

Role

UX/UI Designer

Type

Mobile App

Focus

UX Research · Information Architecture · UI Design

Overview

This project came from a personal place - my partner lost his father in a terror attack. He shared the struggle of grieving in the digital world, so we decided to create a platform for it.

The Problem

The digital world wasn't designed for grief

People end up using WhatsApp or Facebook for something that deserves far more care - there's no private, structured space designed for loss.

Emotional needs are unaddressed
Existing solutions lack private, safe spaces for grief. Public platforms expose vulnerability to unwanted audiences.
Overwhelming user experience
Confusing navigation and lack of intuitive guidance -the last thing a grieving person needs is a complex interface.
No unified platform
No single place combining event planning, memory sharing, and emotional support. People cobble together multiple apps.

How Might We

How might we create a digital space for remembrance that supports shared grief within today's digital culture?

What existing tools offer

I looked at what exists and found the same gap everywhere - tools built for events or social sharing, not for grief.

Platform Strengths Key Gap
Shiva App Practical tools, event coordination Complex, non-intuitive UX and outdated visual design
Social Media Highly accessible, intuitive, familiar Lacks privacy, structure, and emotional safety
Yizkor Web Formal memorial structure Feels rigid and emotionally detached -not human

Core Insight

No existing platform allows people to both manage the practical aspects of loss and come together around shared grief in one coherent experience.

#1

Emotional needs are not addressed

None of the existing solutions offer a safe, private space for grief.

#2

User experience is overwhelming

Confusing navigation adds friction for users already in emotional overload.

#3

No unified platform for memory

No single place combines event planning, memory sharing, and community support.

User Personas

Two users - two sides of loss

From the research, two types of users emerged - the grieving family member, and the people around them who want to help.

Ofek Levy
Primary User · Family Member

Lost her younger sister in a terror attack. Dealing with grief and logistics at the same time.

Goal

To preserve her sister's legacy and feel quietly supported -without having to ask.

Pain Points

Feels emotionally alone; fears being a burden

Overwhelmed by the logistics of memorial coordination

No single place to store and share memories safely

Omer Barak
Secondary User · Close Friend

Wants to support without being intrusive - but doesn't know when or how.

Goal

To offer support in a meaningful way, at the right moment, without overstepping.

Pain Points

Unsure of appropriate timing for reaching out

No clear way to contribute meaningfully

Struggles balancing support with own responsibilities

The Solution

An app to share grief together

I designed MEMORE to combine the emotional and practical sides of grief in one place - memorial pages, events, shared tasks, and private spaces.

Preserve the Memory
A living space for stories, photos, and shared memories -a tribute, not a database.
Organize Memorial Events
Handles the logistics so the focus stays on people, not planning.
Coordinate Support
A shared checklist so the community can contribute -without anyone needing to ask.
Safe & Private Space
Full privacy controls so families can share vulnerably within a trusted circle.

Key Screens

The four main screens

01

Home Screen

Home Page

Shows upcoming events and recent memories in one focused place.

Upcoming events the user registered for
Recent events and photos in story format
Memorial pages to follow and stay connected
02

Event Page

Event page - logistics and community task list

Centralizes logistics so the community can help without anyone needing to ask.

Time, location, and host at a glance
Shared checklist - guests can claim tasks
Event chat, separate from WhatsApp
03

Memorial Page

Memorial page - a shared space for stories and photos

Brings together all memories, events, and shared content about a loved one in one place.

Memories wall - presents the life story of the person, written by family or community members
Events tab - displays memorial events related to the person, allowing others to participate and commemorate together
04

Events Discovery

Events Discovery

Browse memorial events to join.

Three tabs: recommended, nearby, and from followed memorial pages
Each card shows title, location, and date

Reflection

What I learned

I learned that in sensitive domains, good design means removing friction - not adding features. The challenge was knowing what not to include.
